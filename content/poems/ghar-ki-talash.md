mai ghar ke bahar nikla ghar ki talash me to mujhe dikhe'

chand badhiya log,  ghar jaise

char diwariya,  ghar ki diwar jaisi

kuch rasoeyye,  maa jaise 

kuch majdoor,  pita jaise

bahut se dost,  Bhai,Behen or dost jaise

ped,paudhe,hawa,barsaat,  mere gao jaise

gine chune ghar bhi dikhe,  ghar jaise

GHAR KI TALASH ABHI JARI HAI
